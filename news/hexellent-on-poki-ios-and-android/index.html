Hexellent is available on Poki, iOS, and Android

Published 5 November 2026 · By Seal Unicorn Games

Hexellent: Merge Hexa Puzzle from Seal Unicorn Games is available on web, iOS, and Android. Play free on Poki, download the iOS version from the App Store, or get the Android version on Google Play.

Looking for a Hexasort or Hexaslide puzzle? In Hexellent, place stacks of hex tiles so matching colors come together, trigger cascades, and free up space for your next move.
