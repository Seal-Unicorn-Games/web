# Privacy Policy for Seal Unicorn Games **Effective Date: August 22, 2026** ## Welcome Seal Unicorn Games ("we", "us", or "our") develops games available on Poki.com. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect through our games, how we use and protect that information, how third-party analytics services process it on our behalf, and what privacy rights may be available to you. This Privacy Policy applies to data processing performed by Seal Unicorn Games in connection with our games. Poki.com may separately collect and process information under its own privacy policies. ## 1. Games Covered by This Privacy Policy This Privacy Policy applies to all games released by Seal Unicorn Games on Poki.com, including all current and future releases. ## 2. Information We Collect We use GameAnalytics to understand how our games are played and to improve their quality. Depending on the game and the analytics events implemented in it, GameAnalytics may process the following categories of information: * **Pseudonymous identifiers**, such as a GameAnalytics-generated user identifier, session identifiers, session numbers, and individual event identifiers. * **Gameplay and progression information**, such as levels started, completed or failed, number of attempts, scores, game progress, playtime, session duration, and other gameplay events. * **In-game economy information**, such as virtual currencies or resources earned, spent, or used, where these events are tracked by the game. * **Technical information**, such as game version or build, GameAnalytics SDK version, platform, operating system and version, device information, game engine version, connection type, and similar technical information made available to the analytics SDK. * **Approximate location information**, such as country and, depending on GameAnalytics processing and configuration, region or city. This information may be derived from the player's IP address. GameAnalytics may process an IP address for this purpose and may store it in anonymized form. * **Event and session timestamps** and other metadata associated with analytics events. * **Performance and error information**, where configured, to help us identify bugs, crashes, performance issues, or other technical problems. The exact analytics events may differ between games. GameAnalytics considers pseudonymous identifiers and gameplay information associated with an individual player identifier to be personal data under applicable data protection laws, even when that information does not directly identify the player by name. ## 3. How We Use Information We use analytics information to: * Understand how players interact with our games. * Measure game progression and identify where players encounter difficulties or stop playing. * Improve level design, balancing, game mechanics, and in-game economies. * Measure playtime, sessions, retention, and general player engagement. * Identify bugs, errors, performance problems, and technical issues. * Evaluate changes and improvements made to our games. * Maintain and improve the overall quality of our games. We do not use GameAnalytics data to intentionally identify players by their real-world identity. ## 4. GameAnalytics We use **GameAnalytics ApS** as our analytics service provider. For analytics data collected from our games, Seal Unicorn Games acts as the **data controller**, meaning that we determine why analytics information is collected and used. GameAnalytics acts as a **data processor** and processes analytics data on our behalf and according to our instructions and our agreement with GameAnalytics. GameAnalytics may use authorized subprocessors and cloud infrastructure to provide its services. This may involve processing information in countries other than the country in which the player is located. Where required, GameAnalytics uses appropriate safeguards for international data transfers. For additional information about how GameAnalytics processes and protects information, please refer to the GameAnalytics Privacy Notice, Developer Policy, Data Processing Addendum, and Data Retention and Deletion Policy. ## 5. Information We Do Not Intentionally Collect Through our gameplay analytics, we do not intentionally collect: * Your name. * Your email address. * Your telephone number. * Login passwords or account credentials. * Your street address. * Precise GPS or other precise geolocation information. * Contacts, private messages, or similar personal communications. * Sensitive personal information such as health information, religious beliefs, political opinions, or biometric information. Our games do not require players to provide these categories of information in order to use GameAnalytics. If you contact Seal Unicorn Games directly by email, we will receive the information that you voluntarily provide in your message. Such correspondence is separate from gameplay analytics. ## 6. Sharing of Information We do not sell or rent player personal data. Analytics information may be shared with: * **GameAnalytics**, which processes analytics information on our behalf. * **Authorized subprocessors used by GameAnalytics** where necessary to provide its analytics and infrastructure services. * **Government authorities, courts, or law enforcement**, where disclosure is required by applicable law. * Other parties where disclosure is reasonably necessary to protect our legal rights, the security of our games, or our players. We may also use or share aggregated or anonymized statistics that can no longer reasonably be associated with an individual player. ## 7. Data Retention We retain analytics information only for as long as reasonably necessary for analytics, game improvement, security, and related operational purposes. GameAnalytics maintains different retention periods for different categories of analytics data. As of the Effective Date of this Privacy Policy, the GameAnalytics Data Processing Addendum specifies retention periods including: * Raw and annotated analytics data: **1 month**. * Player Warehouse data: **1 year**. * Player Lookup data used for retention calculations: **18 months**. Some information may subsequently be aggregated, anonymized, or deleted. GameAnalytics may update its retention practices from time to time. Current retention information is available in the GameAnalytics Data Processing Addendum and Data Retention and Deletion Policy. ## 8. Your Privacy Rights Depending on your country or region, you may have rights regarding your personal data, including the right to: * Request access to personal data relating to you. * Request correction or deletion of personal data. * Request restriction of certain processing. * Object to certain processing. * Withdraw consent where processing is based on consent. * Request portability of your personal data where applicable. * Submit a complaint to an appropriate data protection authority. Because GameAnalytics primarily uses pseudonymous identifiers rather than names or email addresses, additional technical information or identifiers may be required to locate data associated with a particular player. To make a privacy request relating to analytics collected through a Seal Unicorn Games game, please contact us using the email address below. Where necessary, GameAnalytics may assist us in fulfilling the request. ## 9. Security of Your Information Seal Unicorn Games takes reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect analytics information against accidental or unlawful loss, misuse, unauthorized access, disclosure, alteration, or destruction. GameAnalytics also maintains technical and organizational security measures for information processed through its services. No method of transmission or electronic storage can provide absolute security, but we and our service providers take reasonable measures appropriate to the nature of the information being processed. ## 10. Children's Privacy Our games do not ask players to provide their name, email address, telephone number, or other direct contact information for analytics purposes. Where additional requirements apply to the processing of children's personal data, we and our service providers are required to comply with applicable children's privacy laws and platform requirements. If you are a parent or guardian and believe that personal data relating to a child has been processed improperly through one of our games, please contact us. ## 11. Changes to This Privacy Policy We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes to our games, analytics services, legal requirements, or data processing practices. When we make changes, we will publish the updated Privacy Policy on this page and update the Effective Date above. We recommend reviewing this Privacy Policy periodically. ## Contact Us If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy or would like to make a privacy-related request, please contact: **Seal Unicorn Games** **Email:** [seal.unicorn.games@gmail.com](mailto:seal.unicorn.games@gmail.com)
